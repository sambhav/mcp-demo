# Architecture Decision Record

Write `docs/adr/NNNN-<slug>.md` (next free number) with:

- **Title**: the decision, as a short noun phrase.
- **Status**: Proposed.
- **Context**: the forces at play, constraints, what prompted the decision.
- **Options**: each considered option with its trade-offs (2-4 options).
- **Decision**: the chosen option and the one-paragraph reason.
- **Consequences**: what gets easier, what gets harder, follow-ups.

Ask for the missing facts (constraints, options) before writing if ARGUMENTS don't give them.
